# Facts for review

Each row offers two possible facts for the answer side of a card. The first batch covers five bodies of water and three countries.

| Place | A | B |
|---|---|---|
| Denmark Strait | Earth’s largest waterfall is underwater between Greenland and Iceland. Cold Nordic water sinks beneath warmer water and spills down a drop in the seabed. [NOAA](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/largest-waterfall.html) | In 1941, HMS *Hood* sank here during a 16-minute battle with the *Bismarck*. Only three of the 1,415 people aboard survived. [Royal Navy](https://www.royalnavy.mod.uk/news/2026/may/26/20260528-85th-anniversary-of-the-sinking-of-hms-hood-marked-with-special-memorial-service) |
| Gulf of California | The entire wild range of the vaquita fits inside the northern tip of this gulf. No other whale, dolphin, or porpoise has such a small range. [NOAA Fisheries](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/vaquita/resources) | More than 3½ km beneath the gulf, a research robot filmed what looks like an upside-down waterfall. Hot vent fluid rises and spills around a rock overhang. [MBARI expedition footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6kBN5zLtKkA) |
| Gulf of Mexico | The gulf has pools on its own seafloor. In one, researchers saw mussels and a trail on the slope where dense brine appeared to have run downhill into the pool. [NOAA expedition photograph](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/multimedia/daily-image-media-20211002/) | The asteroid that struck Mexico 66 million years ago shook the far side of the gulf hard enough to collapse part of Florida’s underwater escarpment. [US Geological Survey](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/bolide-impact-effects-west-florida-platform-gulf-mexico-end-cretaceous-and-late-eocene) |
| Black Sea | A Greek trading ship survived intact more than 2 km beneath the surface. The deep water has so little oxygen that even ancient wood can survive there. [University of Southampton](https://www.southampton.ac.uk/smmi/news/2018/11/02-worlds-oldest-shipwreck-discovered.page) | The sea has a second, ancient coastline. Researchers have mapped prehistoric shorelines about 150 metres beneath today’s surface. [University of Rhode Island](https://digitalcommons.uri.edu/oce_facpubs/59/) |
| Aral Sea | Part of the old lakebed now has a desert name of its own, the Aralkum. Wind picks up dust where there used to be water. [NASA satellite imagery](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/dunes-and-desert-replace-the-aral-sea-152887/) | Kazakhstan helped save part of the lake by dividing it. The Kok-Aral Dam held river water in the northern basin, and the water level there rose. [NASA before-and-after imagery](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/north-aral-sea-recovery-7645/) |
| Bosnia and Herzegovina | Vjetrenica Cave breathes with the seasons. Stand at its mouth in warm weather and air blows out toward you. In cold weather it flows the other way. [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/decisions/8596) | Look closely at the region’s medieval gravestones, called *stećci*, and you can find people dancing, hunting, and jousting. [UNESCO nomination evaluation](https://whc.unesco.org/document/141702) |
| Serbia | At Lepenski Vir on the Danube, prehistoric sculptors carved monumental stone faces with fishlike features. You can still see the sculptures today. [National Museum of Serbia](https://www.narodnimuzej.rs/prehistory/lepenski-vir-collection/?lang=en) | On the Serbian bank of the Danube, engineers moved a Roman inscription **and a piece of the road it commemorates** uphill so a hydroelectric dam would not drown them. [Tourism Organisation of Serbia](https://www.old.serbia.travel/en/see-serbia/culture/archaeological-sites/tabula-traiana) |
| Namibia | Some sand in Namibia’s coastal dunes travelled thousands of kilometres to get there, carried first by a river, then by ocean currents, and finally by wind. [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1430/) | Fog is the main source of water for life in the Namib Sand Sea. That bank of fog rolling in from the Atlantic helps sustain animals in the dunes. [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1430/) |

## Second round

Gulf of California A (vaquita) and Black Sea A (Greek shipwreck) are on the cards. Namibia B (coastal fog) is a candidate for the Namib Desert card. The following alternatives are open for review.

| Place | New candidate |
|---|---|
| Namibia | Follow the narrow finger in Namibia’s northeast and you reach the Zambezi. An 1890 agreement between Britain and Germany drew that corridor to give the colony access to the river. [International Boundary Study](https://library.law.fsu.edu/Digital-Collections/LimitsinSeas/pdf/ibs123.pdf) |
| Gulf of Mexico | Off Alabama, you can dive to the remains of a cypress forest. Its trees grew beside a river nearly 60,000 years ago; rising seas eventually covered them. [NOAA expedition](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/expedition/20ancient-forest/) |
| Aral Sea | Rebirth Island lost the one thing that made it an island. Satellite images from 2000 and 2001 show the lake retreating until a land bridge connected it to the mainland. [NASA satellite comparison](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/rebirth-island-joins-the-mainland-1706/) |
| Bosnia and Herzegovina | A medieval Jewish manuscript survived World War II in Bosnia. Sarajevo’s National Museum recounts that it was sheltered in a mosque in a mountain village, then returned to the museum. [National Museum of Bosnia and Herzegovina](https://zemaljskimuzej.ba/en/item/sarajevo-haggadah/) |
| Serbia | At a Serbian family’s *slava*, the celebration of its patron saint, a special loaf is marked with a cross, sprinkled with wine, and broken four ways. [Tourism Organisation of Serbia](https://www.old.serbia.travel/en/see-serbia/culture/unesco-lists/intangible-cultural-heritage) |
| Denmark Strait | Instruments between Greenland and Iceland have measured pulses of deep water sweeping through roughly every five days. Even the current at the bottom of this strait has a rhythm. [Journal of Physical Oceanography](https://journals.ametsoc.org/view/journals/phoc/47/12/jpo-d-17-0129.1.xml) |
| Dead Sea | Some of the black bitumen used to embalm Egyptian mummies came from the Dead Sea. Researchers have traced the material in mummy samples back to its source. [Archaeometry](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1475-4754.2012.00713.x) |
| Danube | Some Danube water reaches the North Sea. In southern Germany it sinks into limestone, resurfaces in a spring, and joins the Rhine. The main Danube continues east to the Black Sea. [Swabian Alb Geopark](https://www.geopark-alb.de/en/experience-geopark/journey-into-earths-history/detail/the-danube-seepage) |
